CORE SERVICES

What We Can Build for You

Each service is scoped clearly so you know what is included, how we work, and what to expect at handover.

New Construction

Full-scale residential and commercial construction from ground up, using modern techniques and durable materials suited to South African conditions.

  • Foundations, structure, roofing, and waterproofing
  • Brickwork, plaster, and exterior finishes
  • Coordination of electrical, plumbing, and finishing trades
  • Quality checks at key stages before handover

Project Management

End-to-end coordination so your project stays organised — from budgeting and scheduling to on-site supervision and quality control.

  • Scope planning, programme, and cost tracking
  • Supplier and subcontractor coordination
  • Progress reporting and site accountability
  • Risk management and snag-list close-out

Renovations & Remodels

Transform existing spaces with structural upgrades, modern layouts, and premium finishes — without losing sight of budget and liveability.

  • Kitchen, bathroom, and interior fit-outs
  • Extensions, alterations, and open-plan conversions
  • Structural improvements and damp proofing where needed
  • Careful sequencing to reduce disruption

Civil & Infrastructure

Supporting civil works that keep sites functional and compliant — from access and drainage to related infrastructure needs.

  • Site preparation and earthworks support
  • Drainage and stormwater solutions
  • Driveways, paving, and external works
  • Safety-conscious execution aligned to project specs
