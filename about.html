OUR STORY

Excellence in Construction Since 2010

We build more than structures — we build trust

Leyano Construction (PTY) LTD is dedicated to delivering innovative, sustainable, and high-quality building solutions. With over a decade of industry experience, we have completed residential, commercial, and related construction projects for clients who value reliability and craftsmanship.

From first consultation to final handover, our approach is practical and transparent. We plan carefully, manage sites with discipline, and finish with attention to detail — so your investment is protected long after the project ends.

Based in Roodeplaat, Pretoria, we serve clients across Gauteng and surrounding areas with a hands-on leadership style and a commitment to doing the job right the first time.
