WELCOME TO LEYANO

A Construction Partner You Can Rely On

Quality builds. Clear communication. Dependable delivery.

Leyano Construction (PTY) LTD is a professional construction company based in Roodeplaat, Pretoria. We help homeowners and property clients turn plans into well-built spaces — from new homes and renovations to managed project delivery and supporting civil works.

What sets us apart is practical leadership on site. We plan carefully, keep you informed, and finish with workmanship that protects your investment. Whether you are starting a new build or upgrading an existing property, Leyano is structured to guide you from first conversation to confident handover.
